The 7-Minute Nightly Rehearsal

Choose one voice. Listen once before sleep. Write three short answers, then take one small action tomorrow.

Night 1: Choose the thought you want to notice.
Night 2: Repeat the same audio. Keep the routine easy.
Night 3: Check in: what felt different, if anything?
Night 4: Rehearse one response you want to use tomorrow.
Night 5: Pair the rehearsal with one practical action.
Night 6: Repeat. You do not need to add more tracks.
Night 7: Review Nights 1, 3, and 7. Choose what to keep practicing.

Your tracker stays in this browser on this device. It is not sent to Sean. You can print all seven nights or download your notes.
